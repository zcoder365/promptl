How it works

No lesson plans, no grading rubric. Just words and a blank page.

1

Get 5 words

Every session serves up a character, job, place, and thing — plus one bonus word worth double points.

2

Write anything

Use the words in any order, in any kind of story. Seventy words earns points; a hundred earns a bonus.

3

Keep the streak going

Come back tomorrow and do it again with five new words. Streaks build, points add up, and the blank page stops being scary.

Frequent questions

Is Promptl free?

Yes. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no ads.

Who is Promptl for?

It's built for kids with learning differences like dyslexia who find writing intimidating — but it works just as well for any kid, or adult, who wants writing to feel less like a chore. There's no minimum or maximum age.

How is this different from regular writing prompts?

Most prompts hand you a topic or an opening line. Promptl hands you five unrelated words instead, which takes the pressure off finding something "good" to write about — you're just building a bridge between five words.

Is my kid's data safe?

Stories are private to the account that wrote them and are never sold or shared. Accounts run on Firebase Authentication, the same system behind a lot of major apps.

Will there be a teacher version?

A teacher dashboard is in the works — it'll let educators assign prompts and see submissions. If you're a teacher and want early access, email me below.

Who built this?

I'm Zoe, a computer science student at McGill. I built Promptl after watching my younger sister, who has dyslexia, treat writing as a chore — the goal was something that felt more like a game than an assignment.
